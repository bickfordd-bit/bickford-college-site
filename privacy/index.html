Privacy Policy

Bickford College Trivia · last updated 4 August 2026

Bickford College Trivia is a private daily trivia game played by an invited group of friends. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, why, and who it is sent to. It is short because the app does little.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your email addressTo create and sign you into your account.
Your display nameShown to other members of your group on the answers list and leaderboard.
Your answers, and any questions you write The game itself. Visible to other members of your group.
A user identifierTo associate your answers and streak with your account.
A device push token Only if you allow notifications, so we can send the daily question. Delete the app or turn off notifications and it stops being used.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no purchases. Nothing you write is public — it is visible only to members of your invite-only group.

Who your data is sent to

  • Supabase hosts the database, authentication and file storage for the app.
  • Google Vertex AI receives the text of a question when a group administrator asks the app to generate an illustration for it, and when generating draft questions from notes an administrator has written. Your answers are not sent. Google does not use this content to train its models under the terms that apply to this account.
  • Expo delivers push notifications, if you enable them.

Your data is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Open Account → Delete account in the app. This permanently deletes your account, your answers and your scores. If you are the last member of your group, the group and everything in it — including any generated illustrations — is deleted too. This cannot be undone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions, or a request to delete data you cannot remove yourself: bickfordd@gmail.com.
