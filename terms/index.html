Bickford College Trivia · last updated 5 September 2026

Bickford College Trivia is a private daily trivia game for invited groups of friends and family. By creating an account, joining a group, or writing a question you agree to these terms. They are short because the app is small.

Who can use it

You must be at least 13 years old. Groups are private and invite-only; you may only join a group whose invite code was shared with you by one of its members.

Your content

Questions, answers, and stories you write belong to you. By posting them you give the group permission to see them inside the app, and you give us permission to store and display them so the game works, including generating an illustration or short clip for a reveal. We do not publish your content anywhere outside your group and we do not sell it.

Be decent

Write about your own shared memories. Do not post anything that harasses, threatens, or exposes private information about a person, anything sexual involving minors, or anything illegal. We may remove content or accounts that break this rule, and group organizers may remove members from their group.

Reporting and blocking

Any member can report a question or another member from Settings → Members or from the flag on Past questions. Reports go to the group's organizers and to us, and we act on them within a day. You can also block a member, which hides their answers and reactions from your view.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time from Account → Delete account in the app, or by emailing bickfordd@gmail.com. Deletion removes your account, your answers, and your scores permanently.

The app is provided as-is. We work hard to keep it running every morning, but we cannot promise it will always be available or free of mistakes, and we are not liable for losses arising from its use. We may change these terms; the date at the top tells you when we last did.
