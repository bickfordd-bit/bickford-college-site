How the game works

Every morning at 6am ET a new question appears about your group's shared college history, and a notification is sent if you've allowed them. Type your answer, and only then does the app reveal the answer, the story behind it, and an illustration. Answering builds a streak and a score on the group's leaderboard, and past questions stay readable under Past questions.

Joining a group

Groups are private and invite-only. Ask whoever set yours up for the invite code, then choose Join with an invite code when you sign up. You can invite others from the link at the bottom of the daily screen.

Common questions

I'm not getting notifications. Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Bickford College Trivia. If you declined the prompt when you first opened the app, this is where to turn it back on.

Deleting your account

Account → Delete account in the app permanently removes your account, answers and scores. If you're the last member of your group, the group and its contents go too. This cannot be undone.
